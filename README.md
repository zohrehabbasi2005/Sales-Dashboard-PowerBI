# 📊 Sales Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Power BI Sales Dashboard** built to analyze business performance through sales, profit, customers, and product insights.

The dashboard is designed with a clean and modern UI, focusing on usability and executive-level reporting.

---

## 🚀 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Sales Overview

Provides a high-level overview of business performance, including:

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Total Orders
* Sales Trend Over Time
* Sales by Category
* Sales vs Profit Analysis
* Regional Performance

---

### 2. Customer & Product Insights

Focuses on customer behavior and product performance.

Features include:

* Total Customers
* Average Order Value
* Profit Margin
* Top 10 Products
* Top 10 Customers
* Customer Segment Analysis
* Category Performance

---

## 📂 Data Model

The dashboard follows a **Star Schema** design.

**Fact Table**

* Fact_Sales

**Dimension Tables**

* Dim_Date
* Dim_Product
* Dim_Customer
* Dim_Region
